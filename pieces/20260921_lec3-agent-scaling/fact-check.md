## Must fix before ship
- **Claim 5** — the CTA line "We just updated Lecture 3… lessons.tellian.io/en" is false against the live site as of this check (still 55 slides, not the merged v6.4/67-slide version). Either hold the post until the site deploy goes out and re-check, or rewrite the CTA to not claim "just updated" until it's true on the live URL.
- **Claim 3** — soften "only 25%" to something that doesn't overstate precision the source doesn't give (e.g. "under 25%," or "roughly a quarter") since the paper reports pass^8 < 25% for GPT-4o/retail, not an exact 25%.

## Flags
- Claim 2 — the course chapter (secondary source) is internally inconsistent about the single-agent token multiplier (4x in `chapter-part5.md` vs. 50x in `chapter-part3.md`, both citing "Anthropic"). The draft matches the correct primary-sourced figure (4x/15x, both vs. chat), so no fix needed *here*, but this is worth flagging back to whoever maintains the course chapter — one of the two numbers in the course's own text is not traceable to the Anthropic post.
- Claim 1 — accurate but a simplification: the paper also reports decentralized (7.8x) and hybrid (5.1x) topologies that sit between the two numbers the post cites. Not a misrepresentation given the post's binary "swarm vs. coordinator" framing, but flag if a more careful/longer version of this piece (e.g. blog) is ever derived — it should show the full topology range.
- No contradiction found against `wiki/topics/anti-patterns.md` (checked; the file's existing entries are about tokenization/language claims, unrelated to these four).

## Verdict
**2 claims must be fixed before ship** (Claim 5 — CTA is currently false; Claim 3 — precision overclaim on the 25% figure). Claims 1, 2, and 4 are cleanly verified against primary sources and need no change.
