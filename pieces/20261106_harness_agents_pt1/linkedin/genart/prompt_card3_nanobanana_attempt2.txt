Flat vector editorial illustration with a bit of crafted visual richness —
NOT overly minimal, NOT a bare UI mockup. Square 1:1 image, warm off-white
background (#fcfcfb), generous negative space, centered composition.

Layout: ONE horizontal row, three elements evenly spaced left to right,
connected by a clean mechanical-looking rod/line (not a plain thin line —
give it a touch of dimensionality, like a small metal linkage bar with a
subtle highlight):

1. LEFT — a circular gauge/dial, styled like a premium analog instrument
   (think a nice speedometer or a vintage pressure gauge): fine tick marks
   around the rim, a subtle inner shadow giving the dial face slight depth,
   a crisp needle pointing to the FAR RIGHT into a solid red-orange zone
   (#e34948 / #eb6834). A small upward arrow sits above it, with a soft
   drop shadow for a bit of lift off the page.

2. CENTER — one toggle switch, drawn as a glossy rounded pill with a subtle
   top highlight sheen (like brushed metal or polished plastic, not flat
   paper). The knob is UNMISTAKABLY pushed to the RIGHT inner edge of the
   track — clear empty space to its left — filled solid dark charcoal with
   a thin light rim, so the "switched ON" state is obvious at a glance.

3. RIGHT — a second gauge, a visual twin of the left one in style and size
   (same fine tick marks, same subtle inner shadow, same instrument-panel
   feel), but its needle points to the FAR LEFT into a solid blue zone
   (#2a78d6). A small downward arrow sits below it with the same soft
   drop shadow treatment.

The two gauges are mirror twins — identical craftsmanship and detail level,
opposite needle direction. Add a little bit of tasteful polish throughout
(soft shadows, slight highlight sheen, fine tick-mark detail) so the image
feels like a designed editorial graphic, not a flat icon-font glyph.

Strictly no text, no numbers, no letters, no percent signs, no UI chrome,
no logos, no human figures, no literal photorealism, no busy background
texture — the background stays a clean, empty off-white.
