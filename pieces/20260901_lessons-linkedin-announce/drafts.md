## FINALIST — C2 (парадокс внедрения → цель курса)

**900 million people use ChatGPT every week. And 95% of company AI projects show no measurable return.**

Both numbers are from the last year — the 95% is from an MIT study of 300+ real deployments. The
gap isn't the models; it's everything around them. The few projects that pay off usually didn't buy
a smarter AI — they picked a problem AI actually fits, rebuilt the workflow around it, and counted
the true cost with the risks priced in.

That gap is what I built a course around. I'm starting to give these lectures and decided to put the
materials online, open to anyone. The first ones are up.
