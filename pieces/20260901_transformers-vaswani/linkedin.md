Today is a landmark date for the AI world — and hardly anyone marks it.

Exactly 12 years ago, one of the papers that started the industry's turning point was published. It introduced the concept of "attention" — the mechanism every large modern model is built on. Building on that work, Vaswani and co. wrote their famous "Attention Is All You Need," which turned the industry over for good.

The full story: https://tellian.io/2026/09/01/ai-day-transformers/
